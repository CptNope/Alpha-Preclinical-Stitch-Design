## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the high-stakes precision, intellectual gravity, and forward momentum of top-tier preclinical biotechnology research. The audience encompasses biopharma executives, venture-backed therapeutics founders, scientific directors, and regulatory compliance leaders who scrutinize both methodology and data rigor. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Editorial Biotechnology Meets Rigorous Scientific Discovery**:
- An uncompromising sense of academic authority married to sleek, contemporary digital product craftsmanship.
- Rejection of clinical coldness or generic healthcare tropes (no sterile hospital whites or clichéd DNA stock gradients). Instead, it evokes high-end architectural laboratory environments: warm stone slabs, precision glassware, laser calibration lines, and crystalline typography.
- Information density is balanced with deliberate editorial whitespace, letting intricate pharmacology metrics, assay tables, and preclinical in vivo cohorts read with effortless clarity.
- Micro-labels and dataset annotations impart structural, publication-grade credibility while high-contrast cobalt and radiant biocatalyst teal anchor the digital interface.

## Colors

The palette balances warm, organic stone surfaces with deep, saturated laboratory accents:

- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Primary Background (`#FBFBFA`): Warm off-white, echoing uncoated heavy-stock scientific journals and microplate surfaces.
  - Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF`): Pristine laboratory white reserved for cards, data pods, and actionable surfaces.
  - Surface Subdued (`#F1F5F9`): Soft cool slate for nested tabular panels, metric callouts, and secondary backgrounds.
- **Typography & Ink:**
  - Ink Prominent (`#0B1320`): Midnight obsidian with an inkling of navy; replaces harsh black to give editorial headlines deep, rich contrast.
  - Ink Body (`#1E293B`): Deep slate for extended body copy and scientific descriptions, ensuring optimal legibility.
  - Ink Muted (`#475569`): Technical mid-tone for footnotes, metadata, units of measurement, and inactive tabs.
- **Brand & Semantic Accents:**
  - Primary Cobalt (`#0D47A1`): Grounded, commanding sapphire blue driving primary calls-to-action, active stage markers, and navigational anchors.
  - Biocatalyst Cyan (`#00A3BF`): Vibrant, energetic cyan indicating molecular activity, active filters, interactive hover states, and live assay readouts.
  - Amber Gold (`#D97706`): Used with strict restraint for scientific emphasis, alert tags, study milestone badges, and regulatory checkpoints.
  - Structural Hairline (`#E2E8F0`): Clean, architectural rule line for section dividers and card strokes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines three distinct functional voices:
1. **Editorial Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Contemporary, geometric, and authoritative. Set with negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to create dense, confident headlines reminiscent of elite scientific monographs and publications.
2. **Expository Copy (`Inter`):** Neutral, hyper-legible, and relaxed. Body copy leverages generous line height (e.g., 30px on 18px text) to facilitate effortless scanning of compound pharmacological studies, pharmacokinetic mechanisms, and assay parameters.
3. **Micro-Labels & Data Readouts (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, assay IDs, genomic targets, confidence intervals, and category tags. All micro-labels are rendered uppercase with tracked spacing (`+0.08em`) to signal scientific discipline and precision data provenance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model enforces a crisp architectural cadence based on a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (≥1200px): 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a maximum content shell of `1360px` centered with dynamic margins (minimum `3rem`).
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` outer padding.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` margins.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Narrative sections use uneven column splits (e.g., 5-column sticky category overview next to a 7-column staggered study findings grid) to avoid homogenous template styling.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:**
  - Component internals adhere strictly to `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), and `space-md` (16px).
  - Inter-card and structural relationships scale along `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px).
  - Major scientific narrative section transitions breathe with expansive `space-2xl` (64px) to `5rem` (80px) offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical, floating dropped shadows in favor of **low-contrast architectural outlines** paired with **subtle, diffused ambient occlusion**:

- **Hairline Framing:** Primary depth is established horizontally and vertically using crisp 1px borders in slate tones (`#E2E8F0`). On warm stone backdrops, this creates tactile specimen wells and scientific module zones without heavy drop-shadow artifacts.
- **Ambient Elevation 1 (Card Rest):** Used for base content pods and publication tiles:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
- **Ambient Elevation 2 (Card Hover & Flyouts):** Used for hovered case studies, active interactive cohorts, and dropdown panels:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 19, 32, 0.03);`
  Combined with a subtle 1px border shift to primary cobalt tint (`rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.25)`).
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual hierarchy relies heavily on flat surface shifting—placing pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers over the warm off-white canvas (`#FBFBFA`), with nested technical parameter wells filled with pale cool slate (`#F1F5F9`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This conveys scientific discipline, laboratory apparatus precision, and architectural stability:

- Standard controls, inputs, data tags, and scientific badges use `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Content cards, assay modular blocks, and media frames scale to `0.5rem` (8px) (`rounded-lg`).
- Modal dialogs and full-section hero callouts peak at `0.75rem` (12px) (`rounded-xl`).
- Status indicator pills and circular navigational step rings use explicit fully-rounded geometry (`9999px`), providing targeted geometric contrast against the predominantly structured rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Cobalt (`#0D47A1`) with white text, `0.25rem` border radius, medium weight typography, and a subtle forward chevron. Hover state transitions to dark sapphire (`#0A3882`) with an ambient lift.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#0D47A1` or `#CBD5E1`, text in Midnight Obsidian (`#0B1320`). Hover fills with ultra-subtle tint (`rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.04)`).
- **Biocatalyst Accent Action:** Solid Cyan (`#00A3BF`) with white text, deployed for key research pipeline exploration and live study requests.

### Chips & Scientific Data Tags
- **Micro Metadata Badges:** Height 24px, 4px border-radius, rendered in `JetBrains Mono` at `11px` uppercase.
- **Status Variants:**
  - *Active Study / In Vivo:* `#E0F2FE` background, `#0369A1` text, 1px `#BAE6FD` border.
  - *Validation / QA Passed:* `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, 1px `#A7F3D0` border.
  - *Milestone / Critical Parameter:* `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text, 1px `#FDE68A` border.

### Cards & Assay Pods
- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px architectural hairline border (`#E2E8F0`), 8px border-radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Internal Spacing:** Standardized `1.5rem` (24px) internal padding.
- **Header:** Features a category micro-label in monospace uppercase alongside publication date or study ID, followed by a bold `headline-sm` title.
- **Footer:** Separated by a thin divider line or generous space, containing authors, GLP compliance icons, and interactive directional arrows.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, surface in `#FFFFFF`, border in `#CBD5E1`, font `14px Inter`. Focus state transitions to a 1.5px border in `#0D47A1` accompanied by a soft `3px` focus glow ring of `rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp, square or circular 18px widgets with `#CBD5E1` unselected borders; checked states display a solid `#0D47A1` fill with crisp white vector markers.

### Lists & Protocol Specifications
- Key-value scientific summary lists display parameter keys in `Ink Muted` (`#475569`) with trailing dotted baseline leaders or clean vertical column alignments, paired with bold numeric values in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Pharmacology Metric Banner:** Large-scale quantitative displays pairing high-impact stat figures (`48px` Plus Jakarta Sans) with small monospaced confidence intervals (e.g., `p < 0.001`, `n = 42`) and descriptive subtitle copy.
- **Pipeline Stage Tracker:** Step-based segmented progress bar featuring hairline connective tracks, active cobalt nodes, and completed status icons for preclinical drug discovery milestones (Discovery, Target ID, Lead Optimization, IND-Enabling).

Longitudinal IVIS Bioluminescence Monitoring of Gene Therapy Transduction

An emerging Boston-area gene therapy biotech required sensitive in vivo validation of a novel AAV9 capsid variant targeting systemic skeletal muscle while avoiding off-target hepatic accumulation.
